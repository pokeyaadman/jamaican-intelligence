# Publication stages and exclusions

1. `owner_verified`: The project owner confirms dataset content. It does not mean independent professional review or third-party consent.
2. `owner_attested_beta`: Limited source-licensed lexical preview. Maintain CC BY-SA attribution, adaptation notes and provisional status.
3. `verified` / production: Requires record-level subject-matter review where appropriate, rights and privacy checks, and release-manager approval according to the strict production gate.
4. `JAM-CULT`: withheld until cultural nuance, source terms and a licence for original project-authored content are approved.
5. `JAM-BIZ`: withheld until permitted use and operating status for each business are sufficiently established. A company website listing alone is not confirmation of current operation.

No sensitive personal information, private submitter contacts, proprietary audio, protected archive images, or permissions records are included.

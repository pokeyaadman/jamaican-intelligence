## Reviewer checklist

For each proposed entry, a qualified Jamaican Creole reviewer should confirm:
1. The spelling is attested and appropriate for its claimed writing convention; capture differing spellings as separate sourced variants where appropriate.
2. The English meaning accurately reflects its context; note polysemy and near-synonyms.
3. The proposed grammatical classification is correct in the intended context.
4. The source list is correctly interpreted; especially check `laaj`, `smaal`, `liin`, `piipl` and `flowaz`.
5. The reviewer is identified internally, with date, evidence and any corrections; do **not** expose private reviewer contact details.
6. The project owner explicitly accepts an updated collection before any entry is labelled owner-verified. No previous attestation automatically covers these 30 new entries.

## Promotion rule

Copy to the public beta only in a **separate reviewed change**, after owner approval and documented expert review when relevant. Public beta rows require the correct attribution and the existing validation gate; production needs the stricter review and legal safeguards.

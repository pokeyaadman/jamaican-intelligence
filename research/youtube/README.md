# Jamaican Intelligence — Speech-source research v0.8

**Status:** public research-source metadata only. No copyrighted transcripts, caption files, audio, video, still frames, or directly observed lexical tokens have been copied from YouTube.

The CSV registers **six individual YouTube video leads** and **two academic/Jamaican-language channel discovery leads**. A metadata check means a page, video listing, or authoritative cross-link was found. It does **not** mean we listened to or verified the recording, established a speaker's home parish or demographic characteristics, or obtained permission to reuse it.

## Inclusion goals

1. Prioritize unprompted conversations and community interviews with Jamaican Creole-speaking adults.
2. Include educational and academic sources separately as context or cross-check material—not substitute speech samples.
3. Record the location **only if explicitly stated and relevant**; diaspora material is valuable, but must not be mislabelled as recorded in Jamaica.
4. Track register and English–Jamaican code-switching without treating Jamaican Creole as defective English.
5. Seek creator/uploader permission and any relevant speaker rights **before** importing, storing, redistributing or training on copyrighted speech or transcript material.
6. Keep links to official YouTube pages; do not bypass video access restrictions, scrape captions or download audio through unapproved means.
7. Minors, people in vulnerable situations and incidental identifiable bystanders require heightened consent screening. Default is exclude from retained audio/transcript/voice-training datasets.

## Current rights classification

- Video YT-0002 (Wikitongues) explicitly describes its recording as **CC BY-NC 4.0** and requests contact before downloading a copy. NonCommercial is **not** open permission for unrestricted commercial uses; recording and speaker rights still need review. Do not import it into a general-purpose AI training dataset on that basis alone.
- Other recordings have **no independently confirmed reusable audiovisual licence** in this register. This is not a claim their videos all use the same licence.
- Academic affiliations or a public video page do not confer blanket transcription, redistribution, embedding, or AI model training permission.

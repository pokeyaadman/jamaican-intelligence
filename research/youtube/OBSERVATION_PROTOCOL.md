# Speech observation protocol — v0.8

## Evidence tiers

| Tier | Meaning | Eligible for automatic lexical release? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discovery | Video/channel metadata and URLs from public listings | No |
| Playback reviewed | Researcher checked a clip via authorized playback | No |
| Linguistically checked | Qualified speaker/reviewer checked the form, sense and context | Not alone |
| Rights cleared | Licence/permission and any relevant participant rights are on file | Not alone |
| Release approved | Provenance, linguistic check, intended use and release sign-off completed | Yes, subject to field-specific licence |

The discovery rows currently sit only at **Discovery**.

## Safe workflow

1. Record the video source ID, URL, date checked and the source's publicly stated title/creator. Do not record identity traits inferred from a voice.
2. Verify whether the item is ordinary speech, performance, music, a scripted lesson, or commentary; differentiate in situ spoken language from teaching examples.
3. Capture a **timestamp in seconds**, not a transcript. Record an *original*, concise observation of the linguistic feature.
4. If a candidate Patois word/short expression is observed, capture only the minimal lexical unit necessary for review. Never paste lyrics, long quotations, or a continuous conversational transcript into the public repo.
5. Include language-code-switching context and notes on uncertainty, but **do not infer parish, ethnicity, age or other sensitive traits from a person's voice**. Use location only when the clip metadata or participants expressly say it and sharing is appropriate.
6. If a reviewer needs a verbatim snippet for permitted research or fairness/legal exceptions, handle it case by case and keep the evidence and clearance private; it must not be automatically published.
7. Record the source's on-page licence and a dated permission decision. Clearly separate creator/uploader authorization from any speaker/participant consent and third-party music/performance rights.
8. External reviewer confirms actual usage, polysemy, spelling, speaker's register and source context. The project owner approves any new publication batch.
9. A separate release check rejects `source_checked_unreviewed`, `rights_pending`, `not_listened`, and `not_granted` from public speech/audio/text datasets.

## Do not collect automatically

- Full YouTube captions/subtitles, auto-generated transcripts or copyrighted audio.
- Biometric voiceprints, speaker identification vectors, identifiable personal contact details or private messages.
- Speech from minors or persons in vulnerable contexts without specialist ethics and explicit rights review.
- Assumed speaker origin, gender, ethnicity, religion, socioeconomic class or consent from a voice.

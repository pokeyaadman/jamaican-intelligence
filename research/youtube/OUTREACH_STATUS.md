## Decision vocabulary

- `unrequested`: no rights contact attempted
- `requested_pending`: outreach sent; no affirmative grant recorded
- `clarification_needed`: reply received, but scope/identity/participant rights unclear
- `granted_limited`: affirmative permission for enumerated uses only
- `declined`: explicit refusal received
- `withdrawn`: previously granted consent revoked
- `expired`: permission validity period ended

The public research register's `not_granted` values mean **there is no documented affirmative grant**; they do not mean a creator rejected our request.

Never copy a granted permission from one use (linking, quoting, text analysis) to another use (redistributing audio, commercial training, biometric speaker models) without a separate explicit authorization. Before any permission-based data release, store the underlying evidence securely outside this public repository and reference only a non-identifying approval token in release metadata.
